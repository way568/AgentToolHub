# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want AgentToolHub to be a respectful and practical open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on ideas, code, documentation, and product safety.
- Protect private information such as secrets, tokens, private paths, and local configuration.
- Point out unsafe defaults or risky deployment patterns clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting project discussions.
- Promoting unsafe public deployment without documenting the risk.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close issues, or limit participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.

## Attribution

This is a lightweight conduct document for an early open-source project. A full Contributor Covenant version can be adopted later.
